Open the Markdown your AI tools write and turn it into a polished PDF, Word document or web page. Diagrams, math and code included, with a live preview as you type.

Markdown is everywhere now. AI assistants answer in it, coding agents write their plans and READMEs in it, and your notes probably live in it too. Plaintext Studio is a native Mac app for reading, writing and sharing it: type on the left, see the finished document on the right.

### Live preview

- The preview updates as you type and keeps its scroll position
- Editor and preview scroll together, so you never lose your place
- Show the editor, the preview, or both side by side
- Switch on page view to see exactly how the document will print, page by page

### Everything Markdown can do

- CommonMark and GitHub Flavored Markdown: tables, task lists, strikethrough and autolinks
- GitHub-style callouts: Note, Tip, Important, Warning and Caution
- Mermaid diagrams: flowcharts, sequence diagrams, Gantt charts, mind maps, pie charts and more
- LaTeX math, inline and in display blocks, rendered with KaTeX
- Code blocks with syntax highlighting for dozens of languages and optional line numbers
- Images, YAML front matter and page breaks for print

### Export that looks right

- PDF with your choice of paper size, orientation, margins and page numbers
- Word documents (DOCX) with real headings, lists, tables, images and diagrams
- Standalone HTML, with images embedded or kept alongside
- Print directly from the app
- Preview, PDF, Word and HTML all share the same theme, so what you see is what you send

### A pleasant place to write

- Formatting bar and keyboard shortcuts for bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, headings, lists, quotes, code, links, images and dividers
- Every formatting action toggles: apply bold to bold text and it goes away
- Markdown syntax highlighting, line numbers and line wrapping in the editor
- Drag, paste or pick images; they are copied into an assets folder next to your document
- Lists continue when you press Return

### Make it yours

- Themes: GitHub Light and Dark, Solarized Light and Dark, Nord, Sepia and Paper, or follow the system appearance
- Typefaces including New York, Charter, Georgia, Palatino and Avenir Next
- Document styles from compact to airy, plus academic
- Adjustable font size and line height

### Folders and projects

- Open a whole folder and browse its Markdown files in a sidebar
- Relative images and links between documents just work
- Recent files and folders are one click away from the welcome window

### Private by design

Your documents stay on your Mac: no account, no sign-in, no tracking and no data collection. Diagrams, math and highlighting are rendered entirely on your Mac and work offline.

Plaintext Studio opens .md, .markdown and plain text files, and saves them as plain Markdown that any other app can read. No lock-in, ever.
