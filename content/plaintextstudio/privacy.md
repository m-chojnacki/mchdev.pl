Marcin Chojnacki Dev built the Plaintext Studio app for macOS. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information by the app.

### Information Collection and Use

Plaintext Studio does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and does not use any third-party services.

### Your Documents

Plaintext Studio works only with the documents and folders you choose to open, create, or export. All processing happens locally on your Mac. Diagrams, math, and syntax highlighting are rendered on your device, and your documents are never uploaded anywhere.

Images you drag, paste, or pick are copied into an assets folder next to your document. The list of recently opened documents and folders shown in the welcome window, as well as your appearance and export preferences, are stored locally on your Mac.

### Web Content

If a document you open refers to images hosted on the internet, the preview loads them directly from those servers, the same way a web browser would. Links you click in the preview open in your default web browser. Those servers and websites are not operated by us and are governed by their own privacy policies.

### Crash Reports

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your macOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These are handled by Apple according to its own privacy policy and do not identify you personally.

### Children’s Privacy

Plaintext Studio does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 16.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-10
