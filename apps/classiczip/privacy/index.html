Privacy Policy

Marcin Chojnacki Dev built the ClassicZip app for macOS. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information by the app.

Information Collection and Use

ClassicZip does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and does not use any third-party services.

Your Files

ClassicZip works only with the files and archives you choose to open, create, or extract. All processing happens locally on your Mac. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and has no network access, so your files never leave your device.

The list of recently opened archives shown in the welcome window is stored locally on your Mac and can be cleared at any time.

Crash Reports

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your macOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These are handled by Apple according to its own privacy policy and do not identify you personally.

Children’s Privacy

ClassicZip does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 16.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-01
